Bespoke installations and services

Modern show infrastructure that crews can trust.

Custom networked audio, control, and monitoring systems for venues that need infrastructure to be reliable, observable, and understandable on show day.

System design and integration

We design and deploy systems that combine professional audio, IP networking, and show control into a single coherent backbone tailored to your room, shows, and crew.

  • AoIP and networked audio infrastructure for theatres, live music venues, studios, houses of worship, and broadcast spaces
  • IP-based control layers using OSC and other open standards
  • Mixed-vendor integration across audio, lighting, video, and automation hardware

Monitoring and observability

Clear feedback makes complex systems easier to run. We build monitoring and utility layers that show crews what matters before small faults become show-stopping faults.

  • Embedded monitoring nodes for signal paths and system health
  • At-a-glance status views for control rooms, racks, and production desks
  • Operational logging and diagnostic workflows for support teams

Commissioning and support

Installations are collaborative and engineer-led. We start from your existing inventory, workflows, and pain points, then design systems that add clarity rather than more boxes in the rack.

  • Discovery and audit of current audio, network, and control infrastructure
  • Concept and detailed design including topology, routing, control architecture, and resilience strategy
  • Commissioning and testing in real show conditions
  • Training and handover for in-house teams and visiting engineers

Documentation and training

The handover matters. We produce venue-specific documentation, drawings, presets, and operator notes so day-to-day use feels simple even when the underlying system is sophisticated.
